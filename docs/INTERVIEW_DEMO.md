# Three-minute founder demonstration

**0:00–0:30 — Why AETHER.** “Matching is a starting point. Commercialization needs an explainable decision.” Show the dashboard: six fictional energy innovators, $57M sought, nine eligible partnerships. Metrics come from records, not decorative constants. All companies and claims are fictional.

**0:30–1:05 — Explain the recommendation.** Open VoltWeave. Northstar and Atlas pass hard criteria. Expand the trace. Explain weights versus probabilities; rejected candidates remain visible with reasons. Click Explain. If credentials are absent, point out the deterministic fallback explicitly.

**1:05–1:35 — Inspect the evidence.** Open Evidence & trust. Show conflicting technology reports, missing deployment evidence and stale observations. Distinguish source provenance from actual verification. Human diligence is still required; no regulator or technical certification is invented.

**1:35–2:05 — Test an assumption.** Open Scenario Lab. Keep VoltWeave, change geography from Europe to India and run. Northstar becomes rejected because its mandate excludes India. Show the baseline/hypothetical comparison and notice that original records remain unchanged.

**2:05–2:35 — Test the system.** Open Evaluation Studio. Inspect 24 independent labels and the ambiguous CarbonArc case routed to review. K=2 returns all nine positives; change K to 1 to reduce recall to 55.6%. Perfect performance on a tiny fixture is regression confidence, not market accuracy.

**2:35–3:00 — Explain the engineering.** React → FastAPI domain functions → Supabase/pgvector. Optional Cloudflare chooses grounded statements, never eligibility. Render free deployment configuration, bounded inference and deterministic fallback keep the demo economical. Close with the next validation step: real partner mandates, reviewer-labeled outcomes and tenant-safe workflows.
